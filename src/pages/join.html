Registration Always Open

Join Albany Pack 3

Registration is ongoing throughout the year — we welcome new and returning families anytime. Follow the steps below to get started.

1

Complete the Online Registration Form

Pack registration is ongoing and we welcome new and returning families throughout the year. Fill out the form to get your family started, and email registration@albanycubscouts.org with any questions about the application process.

Complete Registration Form
2

Register and Pay National BSA Dues

Register (or re-register) with the council and national BSA organization. You will be asked to pay the national fees. The National BSA fee is $230 per scout.

Register with BSA
3

Pay Pack Dues

Pack dues are $120 per year and cover food, badges, and activities for your scout all year. Please pay via:

  • Zelle to treasurer@albanycubscouts.org
  • Paper check made payable to "Albany Pack 3 Cub Scouts"

Please clearly indicate the name of the scout you are paying for in the memo/details field. If your family needs help with registration costs, please speak with our Cubmaster about financial assistance.

4

Purchase a Uniform

The basic uniform consists of a shirt, hat, neckerchief, neckerchief slide, and the pack and council patches — this can add up to $100 or so. Albany Pack 3 works hard to re-use old uniforms, so check with us before buying! We plan to do a bulk uniform buy each year; more information will be sent out.

You can also order online or visit a Scout store (in Pleasant Hill or San Rafael). Be sure to tell them you are with Albany Pack 003, part of the Golden Gate District, and what rank your scout is. The same blue shirt works for Kindergarten through 3rd grade; 4th and 5th graders wear a tan shirt. While there, pick up the handbook for your scout's grade. Shop online at scoutstuff.org, and see the BSA Insignia Guide for patch placement.

5

(Adults) Complete Youth Protection Training (YPT)

Youth Protection Training is highly recommended for all Pack 3 adults and REQUIRED for all parents/guardians of Kindergarten (Lions) and 1st graders (Tigers), and for any adult volunteering to help lead activities with kids.

NOTE: Everyone who is required to take YPT must do it EVERY year, even if your certificate says it is good for two years. Please send your completed certificate by OCTOBER 15 to registration@albanycubscouts.org.

Start Youth Protection Training
6

(Adults) Livescan and Mandatory Reporter Training

The Cubmaster will communicate with you if this is required. Please email registration@albanycubscouts.org when you have completed this requirement.

Questions about joining?

Our registration team is happy to help. Please complete your registration by October 15.
